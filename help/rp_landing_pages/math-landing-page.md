# Math{#section-overview}

The Math section in Adobe Journey Optimizer empowers you to perform precise calculations and data transformations within your journeys, helping you make smarter, data-driven decisions. Each topic—like the 'random' function for generating unpredictable values or the 'round' function for refining numerical data—fits together to give you control over how numbers behave in your workflows. Dive in to discover practical examples and easy-to-follow syntax that will elevate your ability to craft efficient, impactful journeys.
