# AI models{#section-overview}

AI models in Adobe Journey Optimizer help you deliver the right offer at the right moment by ranking and personalizing recommendations based on your business goals and customer interactions. Whether you want to optimize globally for clicks and conversions or tailor offers to specific audiences, these models use advanced techniques like machine learning to make decisioning smarter and more effective. You’ll explore two key types—Auto-optimization for global performance and Personalized optimization for audience-specific recommendations—plus learn how to create models, handle challenges like the cold-start problem, and track results. Unlock the power of data-driven decisioning with confidence and precision.
