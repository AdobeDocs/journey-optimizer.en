# Sandboxes management{#section-overview}

Sandboxes in Adobe Journey Optimizer are like virtual workspaces that help you keep your environments organized and tailored to your needs - whether for development, testing, or production. By using sandboxes, you can safely experiment, assign user roles to control access, and configure shared content folders for seamless collaboration. This section guides you through managing these environments, from switching between sandboxes to assigning roles and ensuring content availability. You'll also discover how to transfer journeys and templates between sandboxes, enabling efficient workflows across teams. Ready to streamline your projects? Let's dive into the details and make your sandbox management effortless.
