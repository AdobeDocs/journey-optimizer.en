# Adobe Experience Platform{#section-overview}

Adobe Experience Platform is the foundation for delivering truly personalized customer experiences, helping you unlock the full potential of your data to drive impactful journeys. With features like audience segmentation and real-time data integration, it empowers you to understand your customers and take meaningful action based on those insights. In this section, you'll explore how tools like the inAudience function work to determine audience membership, a crucial step for creating tailored experiences.
