# Configure landing pages{#section-overview}

Configuring landing pages in Adobe Journey Optimizer empowers you to craft seamless customer experiences with tailored subdomains and presets. Imagine creating professional-grade landing pages that not only reflect your brand but also track engagement effectively.

This section covers two areas:

* **Subdomains** — Set up where your landing pages are hosted. Learn how to delegate, configure, or undelegate domain settings for your landing pages.
* **Presets** — Define reusable configurations (including subdomain and other channel settings) so you can create and manage landing pages consistently.

Whether you use existing domains or new setups, these steps help you align your landing pages with your campaigns and goals.
