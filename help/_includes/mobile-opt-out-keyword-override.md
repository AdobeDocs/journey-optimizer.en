# Mobile opt-out keyword override warning

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>If you define custom Opt-In, Opt-Out, Help, or Double Opt-In keywords, they override Adobe Journey Optimizer's default inbound keywords. To keep the defaults functional, add them explicitly alongside your custom keywords. [Learn more about opt-out management](/help/using/mobile/mobile-opt-out.md#sms-native-keywords)
>
