# AI Knowledge Reference

+++ AI Knowledge Reference

This section contains structured knowledge intended to support interpretation, retrieval, and question answering related to this topic.

For complete understanding, this information should be combined with the documentation on this page. Neither source is intended to stand alone; the page describes the feature, while this section provides additional context that helps disambiguate terminology, intent, applicability, and constraints.

* **TL;DR:** This page explains how a designated approver reviews a submitted journey or campaign and either approves and publishes it or sends change requests back to the creator before it goes live.

**Intents:**

* Receive approval notifications through an email and a Journey Optimizer alert in the **[!UICONTROL Requests]** tab
* Review a submitted journey or campaign's audience, content, and settings
* Approve and activate a journey or campaign to make it live
* Request changes with a message, returning the object to Draft status
* Fix missing approval emails by updating CX Enterprise subscription preferences

**Glossary:**

* **Approver**: The user defined in the approval policy who reviews a submitted journey or campaign and approves it or requests changes. *(product-specific)*
* **In review**: The status a journey or campaign receives once it is submitted for approval. *(product-specific)*
* **[!UICONTROL Approve and activate]**: The action that makes an approved journey or campaign live. *(product-specific)*
* **[!UICONTROL Request changes]**: The approver action that sends a message to the creator and returns the campaign to Draft status. *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* Reviewing a campaign is a read-only step: you can visualize all its settings but cannot perform any action on it.
* Before reviewing a journey or campaign, make sure you have the required permissions.
* If you are not receiving approval notifications through an email, update your subscription preferences in your [!DNL CX Enterprise] profile.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Approve (button) — variants: Approve and activate (confirmation action)
* Do not confuse: "[!UICONTROL Approve]" (open the approval window) ≠ "[!UICONTROL Approve and activate]" (make the journey/campaign live)
* Do not confuse: "[!UICONTROL Approve]" (approver accepts and publishes) ≠ "[!UICONTROL Request changes]" (approver sends the object back to Draft for edits)
* Do not confuse: "In review" (status while awaiting approval) ≠ "Draft" (status after changes are requested)

**FAQ:**

* **Q: Can I edit a campaign while reviewing it?** — No. Reviewing a campaign is a read-only step; you can visualize all its settings but cannot perform any action on it.
* **Q: How do I make an approved journey or campaign live?** — Click **[!UICONTROL Approve]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Approve and activate]** in the window that displays.
* **Q: What happens when I request changes?** — The creator is notified through an email and a Journey Optimizer alert, the campaign returns to the "Draft" status, and the creator can resubmit once changes are integrated.
* **Q: I am not receiving approval emails — what should I check?** — Update your subscription preferences in your [!DNL CX Enterprise] profile.
