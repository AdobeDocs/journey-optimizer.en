* **TL;DR:** The Identities menu in Adobe Journey Optimizer lets users browse the identifiers that make up their customers and explore an Identity Graph showing the relationships between a customer's different identities.

**Intents:**

* Browse the various identifiers that make up the customers in the database
* Understand how an identity namespace and identity value combine to form a fully qualified identity
* Explore the Identity Graph for a particular customer

**Glossary:**

* **Identity**: Data that is unique to an entity, typically a real-world object such as an individual person, a hardware device, or a web browser *(product-specific)*
* **Identity namespace**: An indicator of the context to which an identity relates *(product-specific)*
* **Identity value**: A string that represents an entity *(product-specific)*
* **Identity Graph**: A map of relationships between different identities for a particular customer, offering a visual representation of how a customer interacts with a brand across different channels *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* All customer identity graphs are collectively managed and updated by Adobe Experience Platform Identity Service in response to customer activity.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Identity Graph — Acronym: n/a — variants: identity graph
* Synonyms: "fully qualified identity" = "identity namespace + identity value"
* Do not confuse: "identity namespace" (indicator of context) ≠ "identity value" (string representing an entity)

**FAQ:**

* **Q: What makes up a fully qualified identity?** — Two elements: the identity namespace (the context) and the identity value (a string representing an entity).
* **Q: What is the Identity Graph?** — A map of relationships between a particular customer's different identities, showing how they interact with a brand across channels.
* **Q: Who manages and updates customer identity graphs?** — Adobe Experience Platform Identity Service, in response to customer activity.
