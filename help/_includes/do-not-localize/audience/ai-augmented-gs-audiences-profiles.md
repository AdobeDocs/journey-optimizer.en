**Glossary:**

* **Real-time customer profile**: A record that gives brands a unified, 360-degree view of a customer; key features are real-time data ingestion, identity graph creation, and customer engagement tools *(product-specific)*
* **Audience**: A group of profiles that you can create, manage, enrich, and activate in Journey Optimizer; key features are audience segmentation, enrichment, management, and activation, along with governance and privacy tools *(product-specific)*
* **Engageable Profiles**: The license metric that counts profiles engaged through journeys, campaigns, or decisioning *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* Profiles engaged through journeys, campaigns, or decisioning count toward your Engageable Profiles license metric.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Engageable Profiles (license metric) — variants: Engageable Profile
* Do not confuse: "Profiles" (the real-time customer profiles that give a unified view of each customer) ≠ "Audiences" (the groups of profiles that you create, manage, enrich, and activate)
