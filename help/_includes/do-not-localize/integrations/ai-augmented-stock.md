# AI Knowledge Reference

+++ AI Knowledge Reference

This section contains structured knowledge intended to support interpretation, retrieval, and question answering related to this topic.

For complete understanding, this information should be combined with the documentation on this page. Neither source is intended to stand alone; the page describes the feature, while this section provides additional context that helps disambiguate terminology, intent, applicability, and constraints.

* **TL;DR:** This page explains how to find, license, and insert Adobe Stock images, including similar photos, directly from the Email Designer in Adobe Journey Optimizer.

**Intents:**

* Insert an Adobe Stock image into email content from the Email Designer
* Find similar Stock photos that match the content, color, and composition of an existing image
* License an Adobe Stock image and import it into the Assets folder
* Understand the permissions required to use the Adobe Stock options

**Glossary:**

* **Find Adobe Stock photos**: Option to navigate or search the Adobe Stock library and add an image to the Assets folder from the Email Designer *(product-specific)*
* **Find Similar Stock photos**: Option that finds images matching the content, color, and composition of a selected image; available for all images (licensed/unlicensed Stock images and images from the Assets folder) *(product-specific)*
* **License Adobe Stock image**: Icon/action that redirects to the Adobe Stock website to buy a license for an unlicensed image *(product-specific)*
* **Import stock image**: Option to import a licensed image into your assets after purchase *(product-specific)*
* **Standard / Extended license**: The two per-asset license types you can buy for a needed asset, as an alternative to a credit pack *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* The Find Adobe Stock photos and Find Similar Image options are available to users with access to an AEM Assets Essentials Product Profile.
* If an email including an unlicensed image is sent, the image keeps its unlicensed form with the watermark.
* Licensing and purchase depend on your Adobe Stock plan or subscription.
* With multiple Adobe Stock accounts, you are redirected to the last used Stock ID; sign in to the correct account before licensing.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Adobe Stock integration — Acronym: n/a — variants: Adobe Stock plugin, Adobe Stock images
* Synonyms: "Find Similar Stock photos" = "Find Similar Image"
* Do not confuse: "Find Adobe Stock photos" (browse or search the library) ≠ "Find Similar Stock photos" (match the content, color, and composition of an existing image)
* Do not confuse: "License Adobe Stock image" (buy the license on the Adobe Stock website) ≠ "Import stock image" (bring the licensed image into your assets)

**FAQ:**

* **Q: What access do I need to use the Adobe Stock options?** — Access to an AEM Assets Essentials Product Profile.
* **Q: What happens if I send an email with an unlicensed image?** — The image keeps its unlicensed form with the watermark.
* **Q: Can I replace an existing image with a similar Stock photo?** — Yes; select the image and click Find similar Stock photos, which works for licensed/unlicensed Stock images and images from the Assets folder.
* **Q: How do I license and use an image?** — Select it, click the License Adobe Stock image icon, purchase on the Adobe Stock website, then select Import stock image to bring the licensed image into your assets.
