**Glossary:**

* **Decisioning** *(product-specific)*: offers a centralized catalog of marketing offers known as decision items and a sophisticated decision engine, which simplifies personalization.
* **Decision item** *(product-specific)*: a marketing offer in the centralized catalog.
* **Decision engine**: leverages rules and ranking criteria to select and present the most relevant decision items to each individual.
* **Item catalog**: the catalog you tailor to your specific requirements by setting up custom attributes into the catalog's schema.
* **Collection**: used to categorize decision items based on attribute-based rules. You incorporate collections into your selection strategies to determine which collection of decision items should be considered.
* **Decision rule**: used in decision items and/or selection strategies to determine to whom a decision item can be shown.
* **Ranking method**: created and applied within selection strategies to determine the priority order for selecting decision items.
* **Selection strategy** *(product-specific)*: leverages collections, decision rules, and ranking methods to identify the decision items suitable for displaying to profiles.
* **Decision policy** *(product-specific)*: combines multiple selection strategies to determine the eligible decision items to display to the intended audience. You embed it into your journey or campaign.
* **Decisioning process**: the five phases of setting up Decisioning, from defining your components to inserting a decision policy in your content. You follow the key steps to work through these phases.
* **Engageable Profiles**: a license metric of your organization. Profiles that receive a decision through a journey or campaign count toward it, along with profiles engaged through other Journey Optimizer capabilities.

**Prerequisites and permissions:**

* Decisioning is only available to users with access to a Decisioning related role such as Decision managers.
* If you cannot access Decisioning, your permissions must be extended.

**Applicability:**

* Decision items are integrated into messages and experiences across Adobe Journey Optimizer channels: code-based experience, web, email, mobile messages, push notifications, custom channels, and direct mail for batch decisioning and personalized direct mail exports.
* Decision policies are available to all customers for the Code-based Experience, Web, Email, Push notification, Mobile message, Custom channels, and Direct mail channels.
* Experience Decisioning support for direct mail is a new capability. Previously, the Decisioning engine was not available for direct mail extraction files.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Adobe Journey Optimizer — Acronym: AJO
* Canonical name: Adobe Experience Platform — Acronym: AEP

**FAQ:**

* **What is Decisioning?** It simplifies personalization by offering a centralized catalog of marketing offers known as decision items and a decision engine that leverages rules and ranking criteria to select and present the most relevant decision items to each individual.
* **Who can access Decisioning?** Decisioning is only available to users with access to a Decisioning related role such as Decision managers. If you cannot access Decisioning, your permissions must be extended.
* **How do I assign the Decision managers role to a user?** You assign the Decision managers role in the Permissions product, from the Roles tab and the Users tab. The user should then receive an email redirecting to your instance.
* **Which channels are decision policies available for?** Decision policies are available to all customers for the Code-based Experience, Web, Email, Push notification, Mobile message, Custom channels, and Direct mail channels.
* **Do profiles that receive a decision count toward a license metric?** Yes. Profiles that receive a decision through a journey or campaign count toward your organization's Engageable Profiles license metric, along with profiles engaged through other Journey Optimizer capabilities.
* **Where do I see how the pieces fit together?** Go to How Decisioning works to see how the pieces fit together.
