+++ AI Knowledge Reference

* **TL;DR:** This page explains how to use the Email Designer's ready-to-use modules — predefined, reusable layout blocks such as headers, hero sections, cards, and footers — to assemble emails faster and promote design consistency.

**Intents:**

* Access modules from the Modules tab in the left pane of the Email Designer
* Drag and drop a ready-to-use module variant onto the email canvas
* Customize a module's content inline and adjust it from the Settings and Styles tabs
* Add content components into a module from the Components tab
* Save a customized module as a visual fragment to reuse it across other emails and messages
* Choose among the available module categories and their layout variants

**Glossary:**

* **Module**: A ready-to-use, fully structured content block — a predefined, reusable email layout block — that you drag and drop into the email canvas and customize through the WYSIWYG interface *(product-specific)*
* **[!UICONTROL Modules] tab**: The tab in the left pane of the Email Designer where you browse, search, and insert modules *(product-specific)*
* **Content component**: An empty placeholder that you configure from scratch, in contrast to a pre-built module *(product-specific)*
* **Visual fragment**: A saved, reusable piece of content that a customized module can be saved as, so it can be reused across other emails and messages *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* Modules are not fragments — they exist within the email you are designing; to reuse a customized module elsewhere, save it as a visual fragment.
* When email themes are applied to your content, theme styles are inherited automatically by modules.
* Content components added to a module inherit the module's styling by default, but you can override it as needed.
* Footers include required opt-out and mirror-page links.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: module — Acronym: n/a — variants: modules, email layout modules, layout blocks, content blocks
* Synonyms: "module" = "ready-to-use content block"
* Do not confuse: "module" (pre-built, fully structured section) ≠ "content component" (empty placeholder configured from scratch); "module" ≠ "fragment"

**FAQ:**

* **Q: How do modules differ from content components?** — Content components are empty placeholders you configure from scratch, whereas modules are pre-built sections you drop onto the canvas and customize from there.
* **Q: Are modules the same as fragments?** — No. Modules are not fragments and exist within the email you are designing, but you can save a customized module as a visual fragment to reuse it.
* **Q: Which module categories are available out of the box?** — Headers, Hero, Testimonial, Cards, Teams, and Footers, each with multiple layout variants such as single-column, two-column, and three-column grids.
* **Q: Can I add content components inside a module?** — Yes. Switch to the Components tab in the left pane and drag and drop a component into the module; it inherits the module's styling by default.
* **Q: Do modules follow my email theme?** — Yes. When email themes are applied, theme styles are inherited automatically by modules.
