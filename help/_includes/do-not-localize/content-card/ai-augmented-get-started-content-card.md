* **TL;DR:** This page introduces content cards in Journey Optimizer, a digital messaging feature that delivers personalized, non-intrusive content within mobile apps and websites, and explains when to use and when to avoid them.

**Intents:**

* Understand what content cards are and how they differ from push notifications

**Glossary:**

* **Content card**: A digital messaging feature in Adobe Journey Optimizer that delivers personalized, persistent, non-intrusive content directly within mobile apps and websites *(product-specific)*
* **Persistent visibility**: A benefit whereby cards remain in the app or website until the user dismisses them *(product-specific)*
* **Non-intrusive delivery**: A benefit whereby content cards integrate into the interface instead of interrupting the user *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* Content cards only display within the app or website, so inactive users will never see them.
* Content cards are not suited to time-sensitive alerts, since users may not open the app or website in time to see the card.
* Content cards are not suited to content that is sensitive or requires secure handling that a persistent, visible card cannot guarantee.
* Content cards are not suited to messages that need an urgent call to action, such as a flash sale, where a push notification or email would prompt faster action.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Content cards — Acronym: n/a — variants: Content Cards
* Do not confuse: "Content cards" (persistent, non-intrusive, integrated into the interface) ≠ "push notifications" (traditional, and available independent of them since content cards work without push opt-in)

**FAQ:**

* **Q: What are content cards?** — A digital messaging feature in Journey Optimizer that delivers personalized, non-intrusive content directly within mobile apps and websites.
* **Q: How do content cards differ from push notifications?** — Unlike traditional push notifications, content cards integrate seamlessly into the user interface and offer persistent, non-intrusive updates.
* **Q: Do content cards require push notification consent?** — No; they are delivered in-app or on-site, independent of push notification consent, which lets you reach users who have disabled push notifications.
* **Q: When should I choose a different channel instead of a content card?** — For time-sensitive alerts, inactive users, sensitive content, or messages that need an urgent call to action.
