* **TL;DR:** This page explains how to read the content cards report and interpret its engagement metrics across the Display & click trend, Tracking data, Tracked labels, and Tracked link URLs sections.

**Intents:**

* Open the content cards report from a campaign
* Interpret Display & click trend metrics for reach and unique engagement
* Analyze profile activity in the Tracking data table
* Identify the link labels that generate the highest traffic in the Tracked labels table
* Identify the URLs that attract the highest traffic in the Tracked link URLs table

**Glossary:**

* **Displays**: Number of times the message was opened *(product-specific)*
* **Unique displays**: Number of times the message was opened, where multiple interactions by one profile are not taken into account *(product-specific)*
* **Clicks**: Number of times a content was clicked on in your content card *(product-specific)*
* **Unique Clicks**: Number of profiles who clicked on a content in your content card *(product-specific)*
* **People**: Number of user profiles who qualify as target profiles for your content cards *(product-specific)*
* **Click through rate (CTR)**: Percentage of users who interacted with the content card *(product-specific)*
* **Tracked labels**: Table listing the link labels within your content cards, highlighting those that generate the highest visitor traffic *(product-specific)*
* **Tracked link URLs**: Table listing the URLs within your content cards that attract the highest visitor traffic *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* The content cards report is accessed directly from your campaign with the **Reports** button.

**Terminology:**

* Canonical name: Content cards report — Acronym: CTR (Click through rate) — variants: Campaign report
* Do not confuse: "Displays" (every time the message was opened) ≠ "Unique displays" (multiple interactions by one profile are not counted)
* Do not confuse: "Clicks" (number of times a content was clicked) ≠ "Unique Clicks" (number of profiles who clicked)

**FAQ:**

* **Q: How do I open the content cards report?** — Access it directly from your campaign with the **Reports** button.
* **Q: What is the difference between Displays and Unique displays?** — Displays counts every time the message was opened, while Unique displays does not count multiple interactions by one profile.
* **Q: What does Click through rate (CTR) measure?** — The percentage of users who interacted with the content card.
* **Q: Which table identifies the most popular link labels?** — The Tracked labels table, which highlights the link labels that generate the highest visitor traffic.
* **Q: What does the People metric count?** — The number of user profiles who qualify as target profiles for your content cards.
