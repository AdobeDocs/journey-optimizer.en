* **TL;DR:** This page lists the live, all-time, and channel reports available for Orchestrated campaigns in Adobe Journey Optimizer, covering email, mobile message, push, and direct mail performance.

**Intents:**

* Find the live report that shows real-time impact and performance of an Orchestrated campaign.

* Understand when data becomes available in the Live report.

**Glossary:**

* **Live report**: a built-in dashboard that measures and visualizes in real time the impact and performance of Orchestrated campaigns, accessed from the View last 24 hours report menu *(product-specific)*
* **All time report**: a report fully integrated with Customer Journey Analytics capabilities, standardizing reporting across both platforms and improving data consistency and reliability *(product-specific)*
* **Email report**: the channel report for email performance in Orchestrated campaigns *(product-specific)*
* **Mobile report**: the channel report for mobile message performance in Orchestrated campaigns *(product-specific)*
* **Push report**: the channel report for push performance in Orchestrated campaigns *(product-specific)*
* **Direct mail report**: the channel report for direct mail performance in Orchestrated campaigns *(product-specific)*

**Guardrails:**

* Data are available in the Live report as soon as the Orchestrated campaign is executed.

* **Q: Which channel reports are available for Orchestrated campaigns?** — Email report, Mobile report, Push report, and Direct mail report.
* **Q: Where do I access the Live report?** — From the View last 24 hours report menu.
