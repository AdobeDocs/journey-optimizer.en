# Adobe Journey Optimizer packages and capabilities {#ajo-packages}

[!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer] uses a modular packaging model. Start with the base offer that matches your primary orchestration use case, then add the channel and advanced capabilities you need.

>[!NOTE]
>
>Capability availability may vary based on your license agreement, selected add-ons, regional availability, channel configuration, and implementation prerequisites. Contact your Adobe representative for details specific to your organization.

## Step 1 — Choose your base offer {#base-offers}

Three base offers are available. Choose the one that best matches how you primarily engage customers.

| Base offer | Best for | What it includes |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Journey Optimizer – Campaigns** | Batch, marketer-planned outreach | Audience-based, scheduled campaign orchestration for batch engagement and marketer-planned outreach. |
| **Journey Optimizer – Journeys** | Real-time customer engagement | Event-driven, 1:1 journey orchestration for real-time customer engagement. |
| **Journey Optimizer – Campaigns & Journeys** | Customers who need both | Includes both audience-based campaign orchestration and real-time journey orchestration in one offer. |

### Campaigns vs. Journeys — what's the difference? {#campaigns-vs-journeys}

| Capability | Journey Optimizer – Campaigns | Journey Optimizer – Journeys | Journey Optimizer – Campaigns & Journeys |
|--|:-----------------------------:|:----------------------------:|:----------------------------------------:|
| Audience-based batch orchestration | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| Real-time event-driven orchestration | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Channel add-ons available | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Decisioning add-on available | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

### When should I choose Campaigns? {#when-to-choose-campaigns}

Choose **Journey Optimizer – Campaigns** if your primary use cases are:

- scheduled campaigns
- audience-based outreach
- batch marketing workflows
- marketer-planned engagement programs

### When should I choose Journeys? {#when-to-choose-journeys}

Choose **Journey Optimizer – Journeys** if your primary use cases are:

- event-triggered communication
- post-event follow-up flows
- onboarding and lifecycle journeys
- real-time 1:1 engagement

### When should I choose Campaigns & Journeys? {#when-to-choose-both}

Choose **Journey Optimizer – Campaigns & Journeys** if you need both:

- batch audience-based orchestration, and
- real-time event-driven orchestration

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

+++**Does every base offer include every channel?**

No. Base offers define your orchestration capability. Channel access depends on the add-ons and licensed configuration for your organization.

+++

+++**What is the difference between Campaigns and Journeys?**

**Campaigns** are audience-based and marketer-planned. They are best for scheduled outreach, promotional programs, and batch campaign workflows.

**Journeys** are real-time and event-driven. They are best for 1:1 engagement that reacts to customer behavior as it happens.

**Campaigns & Journeys** includes both orchestration models.

+++

+++**Which channels are supported in orchestrated campaigns?**

Orchestrated campaigns support a subset of channels used for campaign orchestration workflows. Channel availability depends on your licensed configuration. [Learn more about orchestrated campaigns](../orchestrated/gs-orchestrated-campaigns.md)

+++

+++**Is Decisioning included in every configuration?**

No. Decisioning is a separate advanced capability add-on. [Learn how to use decisioning](../experience-decisioning/gs-experience-decisioning.md)

+++

+++**I've heard of Select, Prime, or Ultimate. Are those still the current packaging model?**

[!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer] is now offered through a modular packaging model built around base offers and add-ons. If you are an existing customer using legacy packaging terminology, contact your Adobe representative to confirm your current entitlements.
