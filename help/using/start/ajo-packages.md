# What's available in my [!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer] package? {#ajo-packages}

[!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer] capabilities vary based on your license agreement, enabled channels, and user permissions. Use this guide to understand which capabilities are typically available in your package, and navigate directly to the product documentation for each feature.

Availability may also depend on channel configuration, implementation prerequisites, and purchased add-ons. Select the tab that matches your license model.

>[!BEGINTABS]

>[!TAB Journeys & Campaigns]

This tab applies to customers licensed under the current [!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer] modular packaging model (AJO Journeys, AJO Campaigns, or AJO Campaigns & Journeys).

## Base packages {#current-packages}

| Package | Best suited for |
|---------|----------------|
| **AJO Campaigns** | Scheduled, batch, and transactional messaging across outbound channels. |
| **AJO Journeys** | Event-triggered, real-time journey orchestration and automation. |
| **AJO Campaigns & Journeys** | The full platform — scheduled messaging and real-time orchestration combined. |

The following add-ons extend channel and feature coverage on top of any base package:

| Add-on | What it unlocks |
|--------|----------------|
| **Outbound** | Email, SMS / MMS, push notifications, and direct mail |
| **Mobile App** | In-app messaging and mobile push |
| **Web** | Web channel, code-based experiences, and content cards |
| **Decisioning** | Real-time offer decisioning and AI-powered optimization |

## Package summaries {#package-summaries}

+++**Select**

Best suited for organizations getting started with batch and transactional messaging:

- Scheduled batch campaigns and transactional messaging
- Core campaign and journey execution
- Email, SMS, push, and custom action channel foundations
- Standard orchestration guardrails

+++

+++**Prime**

Includes everything in Select, plus real-time orchestration and inbound channels:

- Real-time, event-triggered journey orchestration
- Inbound channels: web, in-app messaging, code-based experiences, content cards, and direct mail
- Advanced audience segmentation and targeting

+++

+++**Ultimate**

Includes everything in Prime, plus decisioning and advanced optimization:

- Real-time offer decisioning and personalization
- AI-powered ranking and optimization models
- Advanced reporting and experimentation capabilities

+++
