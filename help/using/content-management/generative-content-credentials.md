# Content Credentials in AI Assistant {#generative-content-credentials}

>[!BEGINSHADEBOX]

**On this page:** Learn which AI Assistant actions attach Content Credentials, what this means for images that combine several generative AI sources, and what carries over when your content moves between apps.

>[!ENDSHADEBOX]

>[!INFO]
>
>New laws are emerging around generative AI transparency, and Adobe is working to meet applicable requirements across jurisdictions. Content Credentials are the provenance tool Adobe uses to meet the requirements of these laws.

Content Credentials are durable, invisible metadata that record how a piece of content was created or edited. When you use AI Assistant in Adobe Journey Optimizer to generate or edit an image with generative AI tools, Content Credentials are automatically attached to that image, no action is required on your part.

## Actions that attach Content Credentials {#cc-workflows}

The following table summarizes when Content Credentials are attached, based on the image action performed in AI Assistant.

| Action | Description | Content Credentials attached? | Use case example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Generate an image** | Create a new image from a text prompt, from a reference image, or generate a similar image | Always. The image is generated by generative AI, so it always carries a fresh Content Credential. | A banner image for an email campaign is generated from a text prompt describing the desired visual. |
| **Crop an image** (center or smart crop) | Adjust an image to requested dimensions | Only if the source image already had a Content Credential. Cropping recreates the image's pixels, which would normally erase that Content Credential, so AI Assistant reads it from the source image before cropping, then rebuilds and re-attaches it to the cropped result. Cropping itself does not add a new generative AI action, it preserves the existing one. | A generated banner image is cropped to fit a web page: the Content Credential is preserved through the crop. An uploaded stock photo used as a push notification background is cropped to fit the screen: since the stock photo carries no generative AI action, no Content Credential is created. |
| **Add a text overlay** | Render generated text on top of a background image | Only if the background image already had a Content Credential. Rendering the overlay produces a new image from the background plus the text, which would normally erase that Content Credential, so AI Assistant reads it from the background image beforehand, then rebuilds and re-attaches it to the result. The overlay step does not add a new generative AI action. | A promotional headline is rendered as a text overlay on a generated background image for a landing page: the Content Credential from the background image is preserved. |
| **Overlay images** | Composite two or more images together | If any of the source images have a Content Credential, the combined image carries all of them, merged into a single Content Credential. Compositing produces a new image from the sources, which would normally erase those Content Credentials, so AI Assistant reads each one before compositing, then builds a single combined Content Credential listing every source that contributed a generative AI action. | A generated product image is composited with a generated background for an email header: the result carries a Content Credential reflecting both generative AI sources. Two uploaded brand photos are composited into one collage: since neither source carries a generative AI action, no Content Credential is created. |

## Content types and their scope {#cc-content-types}

* **Images**: Covered. Content Credentials are attached when images are generated with generative AI, and preserved through cropping, text overlay, and image overlay operations performed by AI Assistant.
* **Text**: Not applicable. Text-only outputs of AI Assistant, such as copy generation, translation, and brand alignment suggestions, do not require Content Credentials.

## What happens as your content moves {#cc-content-moves}

Content Credentials travel with the image file. When an image that was generated or edited with generative AI is downloaded or exported from Adobe Journey Optimizer, its Content Credentials are preserved. [Learn more about Content Credentials](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/using/content-credentials.html){target="_blank"}.

Some ways of bringing images into your content, such as extracting an image from a PDF or from an embedded (base64) source, may not preserve the original Content Credential. In these cases, no Content Credential can be read from the source, and none is created for the result.
